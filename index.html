MODEL: PAIRS / MR-01
PARAMETER EXPLORER

Mean-reversion model

INTERACTIVE

The model trades the spread between two correlated synthetic assets. Positions are opened when the normalized spread crosses a threshold and closed after mean reversion.

RESEARCH PROTOCOL

Model specification

01 / Universe

Two statistically linked synthetic price processes. No real historical data is used.

02 / Signal

Rolling Z-score of the log-price spread; each window uses only available observations.

03 / Position

Equal-dollar long and short legs; no additional leverage is modeled.

04 / Execution

Position changes at the signal close. Subsequent returns accrue from the next interval; fixed fees apply.

RESEARCH NOTE / QL-001

Mean Reversion in a Synthetic Pair

This research prototype studies the behavior of a rolling spread Z-score under a deliberately mean-reverting synthetic data-generating process. It is a software demonstration, not independent market evidence.

Research questions

How do entry thresholds affect turnover? How do execution costs change net results? What happens when assumptions about mean reversion break down?

Limitations

Both simulated assets share a common random component and an explicitly mean-reverting spread. This gives the model an artificial structural advantage. There is no calibration to actual traded assets, no exchange order book, and no validated live performance.

Next research milestones

Integrate licensed or publicly permissible historical data, use walk-forward validation, test cointtegration stability, analyze subperiods, improve execution simulation, and connect read-only paper account statements.
